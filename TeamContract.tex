\textbf{Team mission:} We will design, implement, and evaluate an interpretable machine learning pipeline that predicts NFL pre-snap defensive coverage from tracking data, with reproducible experiments, fair workload distribution, and on-time milestones.

\textbf{Roles, duties, and expectations:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Justin Kwinecki:} Feature engineering and modeling: represent defensive alignments in ways that expose coverage clues and work with Zayed on selecting model.
  \item \textbf{Zayed Umer:} Modeling and evaluation: train classifiers, tune them, and determine which coverages can be predicted reliably.
  \item \textbf{William Pol:} Data engineering: ingestion, line-of-scrimmage normalization, auditing and cleaning, game-based splits.
  \item Everyone reviews each other's work, helps where needed and works on milestones together.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Leadership and coordination:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Meetings:} weekly, 45 minutes, Mondays at 5:00 PM on Discord or in person. Facilitation rotates each week.
  \item \textbf{Tracking:} shared GitHub repository, with work tracked through Issues and feature branches.
  \item \textbf{Review:} no pull request is merged into \texttt{main} without approval from at least one other member.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Communication and conflict resolution:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Disagreements or workload imbalance:} hold an informal meeting to discuss and rebalance tasks.
  \item \textbf{Missed deadline without notice:} a written recovery plan within 48 hours.
  \item \textbf{Unresolved:} if a member is unresponsive for more than 72 hours or a conflict cannot be resolved internally, we contact the TA and instructor with our Git history and meeting notes.
\end{itemize}
